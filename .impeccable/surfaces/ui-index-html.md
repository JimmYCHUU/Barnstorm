# Surface brief: Barnstorm workspace (ui/)

Mode: Operate. User: Jim alone, desktop, ~15 min a day, five Pages. Job: see each Page's feed, build tomorrow, check and stamp, export or publish.
Constraints: Carbarn identity (blue #0473EA, navy #061D33, Manrope web, Montserrat only for the wordmark); facts from listings; every post judged in Facebook 1:1 and Instagram 3:4; nothing publishes without approval.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each Page is shown as its own Instagram profile before it exists: planned, built, stamped and published posts in one 3:4 grid. It refuses the calendar-plus-table every social scheduler ships.

OWN-WORLD: Navy side rail, cool paper canvas #F3F6FA, white surfaces. Carbarn Blue only for primary actions, selection and the approval seal. Status shown as small round seals. Manrope throughout; BARNSTORM wordmark in Montserrat Black. 3:4 tiles at 10px radius, one soft shadow level, authored 1.75px-stroke SVG icons.

STORY: Jim opens to a Page's grid and sees what is out, what waits for his stamp and what is planned. He builds tomorrow in one click, opens a tile into the studio to check it in real Facebook and Instagram frames, stamps it, then exports or publishes.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- 56px top bar: wordmark on the left; on the right "Tomorrow · n to build" and the primary Build button.
- 224px navy rail on the left.
- Main area: a row of five Page chips with counts. Below it, a profile header (round logo, Page name, site, four stats) over a 3-column 3:4 grid about 680px wide.
- Right of the grid, a 300px column: "Up next" and "Rotation".

FORM: Page feeds, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 0e776d0d. Signature interaction: Approve stamps an animated seal onto the studio preview and its tile.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
